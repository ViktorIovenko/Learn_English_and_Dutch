FAQ

Good questions

Which languages are supported?

48 at launch: the major European languages plus Mandarin, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, Hindi, Indonesian and Vietnamese. More can be added without redesigning storage.

Can I really learn more than three at once?

Yes. Translations stay attached to the same concept, so a session in one lesson moves every selected language forward.

How is it different from a flashcard app?

Flashcards are bilingual by design — one deck per pair. Here the structure itself is multilingual, and you type the word out letter by letter instead of just recognising it.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes — a mobile-first web app, an Android app, and a Telegram mini app. Native iOS is planned.
